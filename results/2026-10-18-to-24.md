# Operating record: October 18–24, 2026

[Back to Workflow](../README.md).

This is a retrospective count from retained operating journals for **one personal repository**, from October 18 at 00:00 UTC through October 25 at 00:00 UTC, exclusive. It covers the deployed originals; those evolved separately from the sanitized source kits published here. It is not a benchmark of the exported checkout.

The counts below include recorded failures and uncertainty. Private issue bodies, titles, worker conversations, paths, and identifiers are not published. The [aggregate data](2026-10-18-to-24.json) and [counting method](METHOD.md) explain the scope. The raw journals remain private, so these are author-reported operational results, not independently reproducible public measurements.

- **22 distinct workers entered the start flow.** This counts starts being attempted, not completed tasks.

## Worktree cleanup

- **Nine live runs; no dry runs in this window.**
- **1,276 worktree observations across 248 distinct paths.** Repeat observations are expected; this is not 1,276 different worktrees.
- **Two runs completed with holds, five ended at a deadline, and two had a failed outcome.** In the status field, the latter seven are all `failed`.
- Mutation records include **36 `deregister` entries**. A deregistration entry is not a verified count of end-to-end successful removals; other steps and final holds matter.

This is a workload and recovery record, not a claim of unattended cleanup success. The next useful work is to reduce deadline stops and make the recurring holds easier to resolve while preserving the removal checks. There is no measured disk-space or time-saving claim here.

## What changed after real failures

The coordinator's public [fixture notes](../herdr-coordinator/tests/fixtures/README.md) record a concrete lesson: a pending question could be absent from the transcript in one producer version and present in another. The implementation combines the available transcript and form evidence instead of assuming a single stable shape. [Transcript handling](../herdr-coordinator/scripts/transcript.py) and [action handling](../herdr-coordinator/scripts/actions.py) show that lesson in code.

## What this record does not measure

It does not establish business impact, hours saved, the quality of generated issues or rankings, or a complete manual-intervention rate. Model billing is not aggregated into a comparable cost figure here. Synthetic tests, the exported source, and operating journals answer different questions and should not be counted as interchangeable evidence.

For a future prospective record, keep the same fixed window and event definitions, then add operator corrections, intervention time, provider cost with subscription treatment stated, and completed milestone steps. Record the baseline before comparing methods.
