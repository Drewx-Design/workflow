# Measuring future operating results

[Development status](README.md).

This is a plan for future measurement, not a completed evaluation.

1. Identify the exact version and a fixed observation period. Separate fixture tests, development probes, dry runs, and operational runs. Note version changes during the period.
2. Count attempts and confirmed outcomes separately. Deduplicate by operation identity rather than journal-row count. Explain partial runs, protective holds, deadlines, defects, and unresolved outcomes.
3. Record operator corrections and intervention time. If reporting cost, explain what is included and how subscriptions are treated. Do not estimate time saved without a baseline.
4. Follow incomplete operations through recovery and state the cutoff. Report failures and remaining uncertainty alongside completed work.
5. Evaluate the sorter's decision quality separately from its ability to write fields. Compare methods on the same frozen backlog and record judgments before revealing which method produced each list.
6. Publish sanitized summaries with definitions and limits. Keep private issue content, paths, conversations, credentials, and raw journals out of this repository.
